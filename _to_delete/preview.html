Fees & collections

From invoice to receipt without a single register.

Class fee structures, late rules and add-ons issue invoices on their own. Collect in cash, UPI, pay link or bank; the receipt lands on the parent's phone with the school's stamp and signature.

  • Billed vs collected vs outstanding, by class
  • Overdue list with who to call first
  • Printable receipts with signature and stamp
Routine & timetable

Drag a subject onto a period. Clashes stop themselves.

The routine knows each teacher's load, which periods are free and what every class still needs. Drop a teacher into a slot they already teach elsewhere and it tells you exactly where.

  • Per-class and per-teacher views, export to PDF
  • Auto-fill empty periods from curriculum targets
  • Substitutions when a teacher is on leave
