# Anekio beacon case (ESP32-C3 SuperMini)

Two parts, print both:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `anekio-beacon-base.stl` | Tray. The USB-C slot is in one short end. |
| `anekio-beacon-lid.stl` | Lid. Press-fit lip, prints lip-up, no supports. |

Size: outside 27.2 × 22.7 × 9.4 mm (inside 24.0 × 19.5 × 7.0 mm), USB slot 12.5 mm wide.

## For the print shop
- PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 20% infill, no supports.
- Any colour (white looks clean on a desk). No metal paint: metal blocks Bluetooth.
- About 5 g of plastic per case; usually ₹50–150 each at a local 3D printing shop, less in bulk.

## Fitting
1. Push the board into the tray with the USB-C port in the slot. A small piece of double-sided tape under the board holds it.
2. Press the lid on.
3. Plug in the charger cable and stick the case to the desk or wall with double-sided tape.
4. Add an "Anekio attendance" sticker on the lid.

The sizes are for the common ESP32-C3 SuperMini (22.5 × 18 mm). If your board is different, the numbers are at the top of the script that made these files.
