- (decision) Document template design is a laptop/desktop administrative capability; phones support selection, preview, generation, download, print, and share but not layout editing.
- (decision) Provide strong default templates across student, exam, fee, employee, certificate, and letter document categories, plus an advanced drag-and-drop editor.
- (decision) Use separate permanent entity identifiers and per-generated-document identifiers; QR codes verify a document through an opaque token while barcodes support fast internal scanning.
- (assumption) Fast path and launch-level rigor are assumed from the user asking to proceed with all relevant templates and inviting product suggestions.
- (decision) Name the verification capability Cultivate Verify and separate Verification QR from decorative Custom URL QR to prevent false authenticity cues.
- (decision) Issued records preserve rendered output, immutable template version, source snapshot/reference, issuer, lifecycle, and cryptographic file hash; reissue creates a new record.
- (decision) Public verification shows minimal canonical details and status; Aadhaar and direct personal data are prohibited from QR payloads and public identifiers.
- (event) Three research passes completed: India school documents/privacy, secure verification identifiers, and variable-data editor patterns.
