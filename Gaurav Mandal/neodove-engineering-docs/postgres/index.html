Non-negotiable safety rules

  • Confirm the hostname, database name, PostgreSQL major version, and whether the server is primary or standby.
  • Preserve the original data directory, volume, backup, and incident logs until recovery is signed off.
  • Keep backups off the database volume and copy them to approved durable storage.
  • Regularly test restoration. An untested backup is only a file, not a recovery plan.
  • A replica is not a backup: accidental deletes and corruption can replicate immediately.
