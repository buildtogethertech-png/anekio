Questions

Any doubts? Ask away.

The things schools ask us most.

Do you offer a free trial?

We start with a free 20-minute online demo on your own school's needs. When you decide to go ahead, our team sets up your school and trains your staff, so you never start with an empty system.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how much data you have. We import your students and parents from Excel, set up classes, fees and timetables, and train the office and teachers. We stay with you until your school is running day to day.

What if we face a problem or have a doubt?

Tell us by email or call your dedicated contact. A real person from our team picks it up right away, and anything touching fees, attendance or exams is resolved first. Your feedback goes straight into how we improve Anekio.

Can we bring our existing data?

Yes. We import students and parents from your Excel sheets, following your own admission form, along with opening fee balances so dues carry over correctly.

How is the price worked out?

₹5 per student per month, billed yearly, with a minimum of ₹12,000 a year. Setup, training and the parent and teacher app are included.

How do parents pay fees?

By UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay, straight to your school's account. Cash and cheque payments at the counter go into the same ledger, and every payment gets a receipt with your stamp.

Who owns our school's data?

You do. Your student, fee and exam records belong to your school, and access is limited to the people you give permission to.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Teachers and parents use the Android app, and the office uses a web portal in any normal browser.
